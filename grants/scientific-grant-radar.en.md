## Universal grant abstract

**Title:** QMeta: an open meta-skill model for safer agent workflows

QMeta is a classical engineering model that makes AI-agent work auditable: hypothesis branching, risk review, criteria scoring, expert-council evaluation, final selection and conversion of successful strategies into reusable skills. We aim to develop QMeta as an open-source quality layer for maintainers, researchers and teams that need long-running agent sessions without context loss, fact/hypothesis mixing, privacy-boundary failures or untraceable decisions.

**Expected outputs:** public library, bilingual documentation, synthetic evaluations, reviewer checklists, read-only demonstration portal and a quality-metrics report.
